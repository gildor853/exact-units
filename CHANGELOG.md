## [1.0.0] - 2026-10-10

- Exact unit conversion with bigint rational arithmetic and dimension-typed unit ids.
- Large curated unit set (length, mass, time, area, volume, temperature, pressure, energy, data, and more) with cited definitions.
- Affine temperature scales, reciprocal fuel-economy conversions, and configurable rounding for decimal output.
- Zero runtime dependencies; ESM and CJS builds with TypeScript types.
